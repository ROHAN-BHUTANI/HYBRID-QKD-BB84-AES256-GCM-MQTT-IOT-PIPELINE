## 1. Evidence Label Definitions

To ensure academic integrity, every record in the research repository is assigned an evidence label corresponding strictly to the level of access obtained:
- **`FULL_TEXT_ACCESSED`**: The complete body text (methodology, formulas, experimental figures, tables, and conclusion) was retrieved and reviewed. Claims and parameters are directly verified.
- **`ABSTRACT_ONLY`**: The title, author list, publication venue, year, DOI, and abstract/publisher summary were verified via publisher platforms (IEEE, ScienceDirect, Nature, MDPI, etc.). Detailed internal numerical findings not stated in the abstract are not extrapolated.
- **`METADATA_ONLY`**: Only bibliographic metadata (DOI resolver, CrossRef, DBLP) was verified.
- **`PREPRINT`**: Source is verified as an unreviewed public preprint (e.g., arXiv, IACR ePrint).
- **`THESIS`**: Source is verified as an institutional academic dissertation or thesis.
- **`REJECTED`**: Record could not be confirmed in publisher indices or does not exist.
